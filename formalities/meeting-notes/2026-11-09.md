# 2.2 Design discussion

Is 'virus killer' the appropriate setting for the game?
We determined it is boring visually.

What setting should the game be in?

Idea 1: Post-apocalyptic world, main character: bin-man

Idea 2: Robot in a junkyard killing organic mutated monsters
decending in the big junk pile

summary of design ideas discussed:

Junkyard theme is pretty loose, i.e, we can have levels in caves
and other related areas. 

Each level can have a general conistent theme. 
Prefabs placed algorithmically.
Rooms without a clear purpose to the player gives depth.
Atmosphere and ambience is key.

Descending in levels can be descending 3 levels in e.g a scyscraper.
In this transition you can as artist expose lore about the world.
For example that the world has gone to shit. :}

Setting change vote:
Unanimous vote to change from 'Virus killer' to 'Junkyard'
